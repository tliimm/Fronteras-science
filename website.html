HELPING OUR ECOSYSTEM


HOW CAN WE TAKE CARE OF OUR ECOSYSTEM.

1. Reduce, reuse, and recycle.

-By doing the 3 R's, We reduce the amount of waste we produce and we can reuse items as much as we can.


2. Volunteer

- We should volunteer for cleanups in our community. It helps us clean our environment and reduce the chances of ecosystem issues.


3. Educate

- By the help of education, we can help others understand the importance and value of our natural resources.


4. Conserve water

- The less water you use, the less runoff and wastewater that eventually ends up in the ocean.


5. Plant trees.

- Trees provide food and oxygen. They help save energy, clean the air, and help combat climate change.


6. Conserve energy.

- By reducing energy consumption, we can reduce the amount of harmful greenhouse gases produced.
